Our story

Designed in Morocco, worn everywhere

Maison Jawaher began with a simple idea: jewelry that carries the warmth of Moroccan craft into pieces you'll actually reach for every day. Every design starts as a sketch inspired by the zellige, tilework and gardens around us — from there, each piece is cast and finished by hand by artisans working in small batches, never mass-produced, always recognizable.

— The Maison Jawaher Atelier

“Jewelry should feel like it already belonged to you — quietly beautiful, never loud, made to last well beyond the season it was bought in.”

Founder, Maison Jawaher
